Child Safety Standards and CSAE Policy

App Name: FTBS Bible App

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy Against CSAE

FTBS Bible App maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are fully committed to ensuring our platform is completely free from any form of child exploitation, abuse, or endangerment.

2. Prohibition of CSAM

Any content, behavior, or imagery related to Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) is strictly prohibited. We take immediate action to remove any violating content and report incidents to the appropriate authorities (such as NCMEC) as required by law.

3. In-App Reporting Mechanism

If you encounter any content or user behavior that violates these standards, you can report it directly inside the app through our Contact Us / Settings section, or by reaching out to our support team.

4. Child Safety Point of Contact

For any inquiries, reports, or concerns regarding child safety standards or compliance, please contact our designated compliance officer directly:

Email: support@ftbsbibleapp.com
